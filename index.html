How it works

  1. Load today's warnings list (or use the sample)
  2. App sorts items by stockroom location (JJ → 1F → 2F → Attic → Mela → Unit) so you walk one route, never doubling back
  3. App shows one item at a time with the qty needed and which floor/room to grab it from
  4. Staff scans the product barcode with the phone camera
  5. App beeps GREEN and advances if correct, beeps RED if wrong
  6. Skip out-of-stock items with one tap
  7. At the end, download the verified transfer manifest to upload to EPOSNow
